Features

Everything the front desk, the chair, and the back office need

Built specifically for how Kenyan dental clinics take appointments, chart treatment, and get paid.

Appointment & calendar

Day, week and month views by dentist or by room, with live status on every slot — confirmed, in progress or emergency.

FDI odontogram

Append-only tooth charting using the FDI numbering system, so every procedure stays part of a patient's permanent record.

Treatment planning

Build cost breakdowns and payment plans, then track patient approval from proposal through to signed-off treatment.

M-Pesa Daraja billing

Accept STK Push payments straight from the till, with instant confirmation and no manual reconciliation.

SHA insurance claims

Track claim status from submission to settlement, with a full history recorded against each policy.

Role-based dashboards

Admins, dentists, front-desk and patients each get a dashboard scoped to exactly what their role needs.
